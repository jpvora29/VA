# Demo scripts

Timed run-of-show scripts for the 5–6 minute QBR Virtual Analyst live demo.
Open the `.html` files in a browser, or use the published links.

| File | Audience | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| `qbr-virtual-analyst-pitch.md` | Anyone — read-ahead or leave-behind | You want the full business case and the script in one plain-text document you can email, edit, or paste into a deck. |
| `five-minute-quarter.html` | Mixed room — client-facing leads, analysts, carrier | You are showing the tool to the people who will use it or receive its output. |
| `boardroom-cut.html` | Executive sponsors, stakeholders, business leaders | You are asking for funding, a sponsor, or a pilot decision. |

Both cover the same six segments plus a close, at the same timings:

```
01  0:00–1:30   Opening — hook, what you get, scope, money
02  1:30–2:45   QBR Generator (Setup page)
03  2:45–3:45   Canvas — two high-impact pages
04  3:45–4:30   Chatbot
05  4:30–5:00   Minutes of Meeting
06  5:00–5:30   Deck recap
07  5:30–5:50   Close and ask
```

## What differs between the two

- **Five-Minute Quarter** sells time back and error removal. Hook is "how many
  working days did we spend building decks?" Ask is a template plus a data owner.
- **Boardroom Cut** sells coverage, risk and control. Hook is "how many clients get
  a real review?" Segment 03 answers *who is accountable for a machine-written
  paragraph* head-on, and the close is a bounded, dated pilot ("if it doesn't move
  cycle time and coverage, we stop").

## Before you present

Every financial figure is a `[bracket]` on purpose — fill them with real numbers
first. Both files carry a business-case table, one-breath objection answers, and a
15-minute pre-flight checklist in the appendix.

## Known gap

Segments 05 (Minutes of Meeting) and 06 (Deck recap) are **not implemented in this
repository**. If they are not demoable at the time you present, say "envisioned"
once in the opening and spend the extra 60 seconds on Canvas — that is the segment
that actually sells.
