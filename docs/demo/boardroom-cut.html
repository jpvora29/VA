The close

One ask. Then silence.

Six minutes ago we had an empty form. Now we have a full pack, commentary checked line by line, an analyst you can question, minutes, and a one-page read. The quarter takes twelve weeks. Reporting on it should not take three.

Pre-flight — 15 minutes before

A five-minute demo has no room to recover. Every item below has ruined one.

  1. Pre-generate the deck in a second tab. Click Generate live, then cut to the finished one. Never let an executive watch a spinner.
  2. Warm the cache and run the chatbot question once, so the first live answer is not the slow one.
  3. Pick a carrier with a story — real growth in one market, a clear weakness in another. A flat portfolio makes a strong tool look ordinary.
  4. Check no competitor name is on screen anywhere. One named peer and the next twenty minutes are about governance.
  5. Know who signs off and aim segment 03 and the recap straight at them.
  6. Pre-load the transcript and the recap output. Those segments are thirty seconds each. They cannot survive a file dialog.
  7. Have a fallback. If the demo dies, talk through the accountability argument in segment 03 from memory. That is the part that sells.
  8. Rehearse the close word for word. Sponsor, market, carrier, one day, measured on cycle time and coverage. Then stop.
