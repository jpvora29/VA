# When the deck covers several markets

The overall pages are the only place the carrier's leadership sees every market side by
side. Use that. A column that only repeats the combined total wastes the one page that can
compare countries.

- **Give the holistic view first:** which market is carrying the result and which is
  holding it back, using the `market.*` facts (each is one country's premium, movement and
  share).
- **Compare, don't list.** "Singapore grew 14% while Japan fell 6%, so the region's growth
  is really one market's" beats three bullets reading out each country.
- **Say where the total hides a problem.** A flat total can be one market up and another
  down; that is the finding.
- **Each field takes a different market** where the editorial brief hands it one — the
  per-country pages that follow will go deep; the overall page sets up the comparison.
- Keep country names exactly as the evidence writes them.
