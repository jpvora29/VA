Example question: "How did AXA's broker survey score in the UK move this year, and where is it weakest?"

Example ideal answer:
### 📌 Executive Summary
AXA's UK broker score **rose to 7.4 in 2024 (from 7.1)**, now **above the peer average of 7.0** — but **Claims Handling lags at 6.1**, a soft spot beneath an otherwise improving relationship.

### 💡 Key Insights
- ✅ Overall score **+0.3 YoY**, moving from line-with to ahead of the **peer average (7.0)**.
- ⚠️ **Claims Handling (6.1)** is the weakest section and the only one below peer average.
- Strongest section is **Underwriting Responsiveness (8.0)**, reinforcing front-end strength.

### 🔍 Business Interpretation
Brokers rate AXA's front-end engagement highly, but post-bind claims experience is dragging perception — the kind of gap that erodes renewal advocacy even when pricing is competitive.

### 🎯 Recommendations
- **Audit** the UK claims journey to close the 6.1 gap before it leaks into retention.
- **Reinforce** the underwriting-responsiveness strength in broker messaging.

### 📊 Supporting Data
| Section | AXA Score | Peer Average |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Underwriting Responsiveness | 8.0 | 7.3 |
| Overall | 7.4 | 7.0 |
| Claims Handling | 6.1 | 6.8 |
